The issues are connected. The work too often is not.

Gender-based violence cannot be separated from economic exclusion, school dropout, climate vulnerability, mental health, domestic work, access to justice, or who gets to tell the public story about women’s rights.

A place to join expertise and resources

Movements are still divided by thematic mandates, funding silos and institutional boundaries. POWER360 is where organizations can act together and build collective power that no single organization can hold alone.

Feminist organizing connected with climate resilience and the lives of smallholder farmers.

Education and STEM

School retention, girls’ education rights and pathways into STEM and leadership.

Narrative power

Shifting who controls the public story about women, rights and equality.

Governance built for shared power

A Coordination Council of all member organizations holds strategic oversight. Thematic teams form around shared challenges. Women, girls, activists and community representatives have structured ways to influence priorities.

The Power Rule. Important decisions should be examined through at least three lenses: feminist rights and power, allied-movement expertise, and the lived realities of grassroots women and girls.
