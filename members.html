The Hub

Member organizations

Who makes up the Hub

Hakizetu Organization

Programmatic Lead. Economic opportunity and movement infrastructure.

Provides programmatic coordination, convenes members, leads shared strategy and narrative reporting, and brings expertise in economic opportunities for young women and girls who are survivors of gender-based violence.

Adilisha Child and Youth Development Organization

Advocacy Lead. Girls in STEM education and leadership.

Promotes girls’ education rights and participation in STEM subjects and future pathways, and coordinates the Hub’s collective advocacy.

WoteSawa

Financial Lead. Domestic workers’ rights.

Receives and manages grant resources, leads financial accountability and partner disbursement, supports financial capacity, and brings expertise on the rights and protection of domestic workers.

Women Action

Women Action

Monitoring, evaluation and learning. Access to justice.

Leads the Hub’s MEL function with Hakizetu and WoteSawa, supports shared learning and evidence use, and contributes legal support and access-to-justice expertise.

Nourish Africa

Climate and women’s economic justice.

Connects feminist organizing with climate resilience and economic justice, particularly the experiences of women and girls who are smallholder farmers.

Girls First Initiative

Girls’ education and school retention.

Brings expertise on education equality, school retention, participation and the barriers that push girls out of school.

Afya Plus

Mental health, healing and wellbeing.

Integrates mental health and psychosocial wellbeing into feminist movement-building, particularly for women and girls affected by gender-based violence.

Ni Hekima Pekee

Sport for social change and community mobilization.

Uses sport as a platform to prevent gender-based violence, challenge harmful norms, engage young people and mobilize communities for social change.

A cross-movement community of practice for collective power, bringing feminist organizations and allied social justice actors together in Tanzania.
